How the pilot's "suggested rate" is calculated

For each pilot item, we look at all other items in the same category (AL1 foil, AL2 conductor/strip, CU1 foil, CU2 strip) currently priced within ±15% of today's raw-metal rate — a proxy for "recently updated, trustworthy" prices. The median premium those items carry over raw metal becomes that category's learned conversion premium, applied to today's metal price. Categories with few or no near-market items are marked Low/Unverified confidence — treat those suggested rates as directional only.

IEEMA's published Price Variation base-price/index for copper and aluminium (the mechanism your industry already uses to settle transformer/cable price-variation claims) remains the recommended long-term anchor, especially once copper items are no longer universally stale. Pulling the current circular from your IEEMA membership access and cross-checking a few items would raise confidence meaningfully.
